## Role 2: Head of Inventory, Akij Resources

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Position** | Head of Inventory, Akij Resources |
| **Department** | Supply Chain Management (SCM) |
| **Section** | Inventory Management |
| **Reports To** | Chief Supply Chain Officer / Head of Supply Chain |

### Purpose
Lead the end-to-end inventory management strategy across AKIJ Resource Limited's multi-SBU operations. Ensure optimal stock levels, minimize inventory holding costs, prevent stockouts and overstock situations, and drive data-driven decision-making through advanced analytics. Work cross-functionally with procurement, production, sales, and finance to align inventory plans with business objectives.

### Key Responsibilities
1. Develop comprehensive inventory management strategy across all SBUs
2. Establish optimal inventory levels (safety stock, reorder points, EOQ) for RM, WIP, and FG
3. Lead demand forecasting with sales and production teams
4. Design and monitor inventory KPIs (OTIF, inventory turnover, fill rate, stock aging, shrinkage)
5. Drive ERP/SAP-based inventory control with real-time visibility
6. Manage slow-moving, obsolete, and near-expiry stock liquidation
7. Lead cycle count programs and annual physical inventory audits (99%+ accuracy)
8. Develop inventory policies, SOPs, and governance frameworks
9. Collaborate with procurement and logistics on supplier lead times and inbound schedules
10. Build and mentor high-performing inventory management team
